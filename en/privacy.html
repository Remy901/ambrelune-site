Privacy policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

In short: Ambrelune collects no data. Everything happens on your device.

Data collected

None. Ambrelune does not collect, store on a server or share any personal data. The app contains no ads, no analytics and no trackers, and sends nothing over the Internet. It works fully offline.

Data kept on your device

Ambrelune only keeps, on your device, whether you have already seen the tutorial and your language choice. This information never leaves your device and is deleted with the app.

Contacting us

In the app, the support button opens this website's support page in your browser. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages: visiting it is subject to GitHub's privacy policy, as with any website. The app itself transmits nothing.

If you email us, we use your address only to reply to you, and never share it with anyone.

Children

Since Ambrelune collects no data, it can be safely used by the whole family.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published on this page, with its update date.
