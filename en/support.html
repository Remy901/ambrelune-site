Frequently asked questions

The sound stops when I lock my iPhone.

Ambrelune keeps playing with the screen locked. If the sound stops, make sure the app was not closed from the app switcher, and that Low Power Mode or a Focus mode is not cutting the audio. The ring/silent switch has no effect on Ambrelune.

How do I mix several sounds?

Simply tap several tiles: each sound you tap joins the mix. A slider appears under each active sound to adjust its volume. Tap a tile again to remove that sound.

How does the timer work?

Tap “Timer” at the bottom left and choose a duration. During the last minute, the sound fades out gently, then stops on its own. The ring around the moon shows the remaining time.

What is sleep mode?

Twenty seconds after your last action, or when you tap the moon at the top right, the interface fades away to leave only a starry sky, with no harsh light. Tap anywhere on the screen to bring it back. The sound keeps playing.

How do I change the language?

By default, Ambrelune follows your device language. To change it, tap the support icon at the top right, then “Language”, and choose English or French.

How do I replay the tutorial?

Tap the support icon at the top right, then “Replay the tutorial”.

I bought the app on iPhone. Do I need to buy it again on Mac?

No. Ambrelune is a universal purchase: with the same Apple Account, download it for free on your Mac from the Mac App Store.

Is there a subscription or ads?

No. Ambrelune is a one-time purchase. There is no subscription, no ads, no in-app purchase, and no account to create.

A sound I'd love to hear is missing.

Tell us at the address above! In the app, the support icon at the top right, then “Help and contact”, brings you back to this page.
